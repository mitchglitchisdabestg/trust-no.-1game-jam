Trust NO.1



You wake up in a cold, concrete basement room.
You find a cassette tape in front of you, and realize you've been here before.

In this (psychological horror) puzzle game, you'll have to use your wits to solve puzzles.
------------
KEY FEATURES:

Psychological Horror and Suspense:
Navigate dimly lit, uncanny 3-dimensional chambers as an atmosphere of fight, darkness, and unnerving audio.

The Voice Memos:
Find audio tapes of previous iterations of yourself, trying to help…

Compelling Console Puzzles:
You’ll have to navigate using interactive terminal controls to fix wiring and do memory puzzles.

The Reset Loop:
After completing each room and reaching the security door, witness your world collapse to the point of restarting all over again.
